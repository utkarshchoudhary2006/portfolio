About

I'm currently in my second year of a B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning at Lovely Professional University, three semesters in and still enjoying the part where a model finally converges.

My interests sit at the intersection of applied machine learning and the systems that keep software reliable — databases, networks, and the quieter engineering work that doesn't show up in a demo but decides whether the demo works at all. I build in Python most days, reach for C/C++ when I want to understand what's actually happening under the hood, and I'm equally at home writing a SQL query as I am tuning a neural network.

Outside of coursework, I like picking a small, well-scoped project — a digit classifier, a recommender, a subnetting tool — and building it end to end, because that's usually where the real learning happens.
